## Sideways Friction
**Purpose:** How does grip work and how do different variables impact it
**Assumptions:** Flat road, no banking
Steady speed, so no braking or acceleration mid-corner
Point mass
Constant μ
No aerodynamic forces
**Derivation:** the force of friction is given by $μ N$ or $μ mg$. the centripedal force is Lateral tire friction, pointing toward the center of the corner, at the tire contact patches. this is given by $m(v^2)/r$. when you set the 2 equal to each other and solve for v you find the maxiμm velocity that a car can take through a corner while keeping grip. m cancels out so the mass does not have an impact in this case
**Result:** $v = sqrt(r μ g)$
**Example:** example: if μ = 0.9 and r = 20m the vmax is 47.81km/hour
**Limitations:** mass stops cancelling once downforce or tire load sensitivity are included
